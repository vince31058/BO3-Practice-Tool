Moon Soft Patch (standalone mod) - by Vinnie
============================================

What it does (Moon only; other maps are untouched):
 - Gums: Immolation, Reign, Perkaholic, Dead of Nuclear Winter, Shopping Free.
   First gum is a coin flip between Reign Drops and Shopping Free; the rest is random.
 - Excavators: Pi (Tunnel 6) is the only one that can start until it has started once.
   It can still start early by chance. After Pi, all excavators are normal.
 - Box: Gersh Device guaranteed by the 10th hit. Then Wave Gun and QEDs both within the
   next 10 hits. Once the sphere is in the pyramid, the box gives a Gersh Device every hit
   (when you can take one) so you can try to lock the box.
 - Timer and splits at the top left: Power On, Samantha Says, Terminals, Vril Sphere,
   Canister #1, Canister #2, Earth Boom. The timer starts when you spawn in and stops
   when the Earth explodes.

Building it (needs the Black Ops III Mod Tools):
 1. Copy this "moon_soft_patch" folder into  <Black Ops III>\mods\
 2. Open the Mod Tools Launcher, select moon_soft_patch under Mods, tick "Compile" and
    "Link", and build.
 3. Launch it from the game's Mods menu, then play Moon.

Note: like other mods that replace scripts\zm\_load.gsc (e.g. preset gums), this mod
can't be loaded at the same time as another mod that replaces _load.gsc.
